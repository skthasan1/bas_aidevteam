# Promotion candidates

Purpose: a log of things this team learned that might belong in the shared kit, so other teams get them too. The tech lead reads it in a weekly promotion pass (see docs/MAINTAINING.md in the kit). An entry is a candidate until a person decides.

Write an entry when a change to your own rules, roles, templates or tools was worth making. Keep it factual and short. Names, projects, keys and incident details may stay here (this file is private to your hub); the pass removes them before anything goes to the kit.

## Format

One block per entry, newest first:

- Date: when the change was made.
- What changed: the file or rule, in one line.
- Why: what went wrong or what was missing, in plain words.
- Where it applies: which roles, tools or templates would need it.
- Generalised rule: one or two sentences with no names, projects or dates, and the reason in the same sentence.
- Status: candidate, promoted (add the kit pull request and version) or rejected (add why).

### Example (delete this block)
